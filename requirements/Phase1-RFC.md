# Lab Assignment – Project RFC

## Objective

Prepare a short RFC (Request for Comments) describing your proposed semester project.

This assignment is **not graded**, but it is **mandatory** and must be submitted by the **end of Week 12**.

The purpose of this RFC is to:

- clearly define your project direction,

- identify the problem you want to solve,

- connect your work with existing research,

- and establish the dataset and methodology you plan to use.

---

# Submission Requirements

- Format: Markdown (`.md`)

- Length: 1–2 pages maximum

- Team submission: one RFC per team

---

# RFC Structure

## 1. Introduction

Provide a short description of the application domain and motivation behind the project.

Questions to answer:

- What is the project about?

- Why is the problem relevant or interesting?

---

## 2. Problem Definition

Clearly define the problem you want to solve.

Include:

- input/output definition,

- expected task type (classification, regression, generation, recommendation, etc.),

- constraints or challenges.

---

## 3. State of the Art

Present a concise overview of existing approaches related to your problem.

Include:

- 2–5 references (papers, articles, repositories, benchmarks, etc.),

- a short explanation of current methods or systems.

---

## 4. Proposed Solution

Describe your planned approach.

Include:

- proposed architecture or pipeline,

- algorithms/models/frameworks you plan to use,

- expected workflow.

---

## 5. Dataset

Provide the dataset reference(s).

Include:

- dataset name,

- source/link,

- short description,

- expected preprocessing steps.

---

# Deliverable

Submit:

- `RFC.md`

- optional figures/images if needed

The RFC should be concise, technical, and clearly structured.
